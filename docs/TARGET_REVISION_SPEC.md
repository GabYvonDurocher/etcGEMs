The independently sampled f_metab is unused on this path. Preserve a separate independent diagnostic coordinate and the Beta(3,1) positive control in dedicated validation runs. Wiring f_metab or f_sector(T) into allocation is a separate biological hypothesis, requiring explicit approval, physiological justification and a different target identifier.

## 2. Define infeasibility from the observation model

Currently `calibration_multi.py` uses `isfinite(o2) & (o2 > 0)` for the clamp support. A missing prediction receives **zero respiration contribution**. The zero-growth/no-scored-O2 stratum has log L at most −18.68250229422251, attained at disc_growth = 1.1407216216373017 (`stratum.json`). This is an algebraic maximum, not a newly solved biological point. D44's saved baseline has a failed 15°C solve and eleven optimal temperatures; its total old log L is −17.972547016139384 and its 15°C respiration contribution is zero.

Proposed rule: each measured observation must have an explicit predictive distribution. First distinguish a biologically justified structural zero from an unresolved numerical solve. A numerical failure is a failed evaluation requiring the registered bounded diagnostic/retry procedure; do not treat it as physical zero, silently skip its datum, or accept a fit containing unresolved evaluations. If valid model constraints imply no biological solution, the PI must approve how that model state enters the generative observation model. Scoring all infeasibility as physical zero without this distinction is not approved.

**Old/new likelihood examples at those saved states.** At the D44 baseline, all eleven valid-temperature terms stay unchanged under a missing-state-only revision. At 15°C, the old contribution is 0; the proposed contribution is either a justified structural-zero observation density or an explicitly unresolved evaluation, never the old free omission. If the measurement is represented on its original scale as y ~ Normal(r,s²), with externally established s and a justified r=0, the new per-observation contribution is `−0.5*((y/s)^2 + log(2*pi*s^2))`. For a five-standard-error positive observation (y/s=5), this is **−12.5 − log(s*sqrt(2*pi))**, versus 0 under omission. This arithmetic is illustrative, not an estimate of the archived experiment's s and not a proposed fixed penalty. At an all-zero saved stratum state the analogous sum is added to its saved growth term; if its solves remain unresolved the new total is undefined pending resolution, not −32 by fiat. A lognormal epsilon substitution is not justified by the fact that the old code logs respiration. For detection-limited measurements, derive a censored probability using the measured detection limit instead. The PI must choose from the actual measurement process, including normalisation terms and units, before seeing any revised posterior.

**Validation after approval.** Archive a datum-by-datum table of measured values, uncertainty, detection status, solver classification, predicted value and old/new contributions at the saved baseline, all saved infeasible examples, and saved feasible nongrowing and growing examples. Reconcile sums to total log L; demonstrate no positive measurement escapes because its prediction is NaN. Test limiting zero predictions, detection limits, measurement units, repeatability, bounded retry failures, and valid non-growing maintenance respiration. Save the exact observation-model derivation and external uncertainty provenance in a commit first. Do not tune penalties to a preferred living fraction, evidence value, or posterior appearance.
