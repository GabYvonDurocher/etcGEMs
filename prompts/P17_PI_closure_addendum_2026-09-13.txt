TASK 2 - write the rigour protocol down, so it survives the handover
P17 enforced a discipline that has not been written as a standing rule anywhere. Put it in
docs/RIGOUR.md, one entry each, with the D-number where it was applied, covering at least:
  - rules and thresholds pre-registered and committed BEFORE the data they judge are computed;
  - never moving a threshold after seeing its data; when a criterion is wrong, correct it by
    argument that predates the data and record the correction;
  - every outcome retained, including failures and adverse arms; no selection of flattering
    results, seeds or parents;
  - interpretations withdrawn by dated correction when their basis falls, never by editing;
  - "necessary, not sufficient": nuisance recovery, evidence agreement, and toy passes are each
    explicitly insufficient as certification (D26, D38, D40);
  - reserved confirmation seeds never consumed during development;
  - independent audit of every completed batch against saved hashes before interpretation;
  - enforced deadlines (SIGALRM), bounded batches, one expensive job at a time;
  - scope preserved inside a diagnostic: no change to the target it diagnoses;
  - an exact blocker recorded rather than a manufactured decisive answer.
Then add one line to docs/OPEN_ITEMS.md section 4 pointing at it, and to section 0c stating
that the reconciliation rule and RIGOUR.md together govern every subsequent run.

TASK 3 - prepare the target-revision specification (PREPARE; do not run)
Write docs/TARGET_REVISION_SPEC.md as a pre-registered specification for a separate
investigation, to be run in Claude Code. It is a proposal for PI approval, not an authorisation.
Three items, each concrete and reviewable, each with old-versus-new likelihood examples at
saved points and the checks that would show the change did what it claims:
  1. Resolve f_metab consistently with configuration D's intended biology. If it is
     intentionally unused, remove it from production calibration while retaining a dedicated
     independent diagnostic coordinate. Wiring it into allocation is a biological hypothesis
     change and must be identified explicitly as one.
  2. Resolve infeasibility consistently across observations. Failed predictions must not escape
     the respiration contribution merely because they are missing. Distinguish a biologically
     predicted zero from an unresolved numerical solve. Derive and document the treatment from
     the observation model BEFORE inspecting any revised posterior; do not choose a penalty to
     obtain a preferred living fraction.
  3. Investigate the seven curvature-sensitive axes (dTopt, topt_scale, dCp_scale, tm_scale,
     kcat_scale, sigma, clearance_mult). Trace physiological switches, changes in the binding
     constraint set, and numerical behaviour. Preserve scientifically justified sharp
     transitions rather than smoothing them merely to help inference.
Pre-register the validation for the revised target: the inactive null AND the Beta control AND
independent-run consistency of active distributions, region occupancy and feasibility-aware
predictive checks. Neither nuisance recovery nor evidence agreement alone is sufficient.
State explicitly that revised posterior fits launch only after PI approval of this document.
